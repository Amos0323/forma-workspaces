# Image provenance

Phase 2 adds two original code-authored SVG spatial diagrams in `public/images/concepts/`. They share an identical room outline and compare fictional furniture/layout proposals. They do not show a real renovation, building, measured project plan or commissioned work. Existing licensed photographs are reused as explicitly labelled architectural references in galleries and articles. Material palette swatches are conceptual colours, not verified product samples. No new third-party assets were downloaded.

All depicted projects are fictional. Stock photography is illustrative and is not presented as commissioned FORMA work.

After the South African repositioning, the existing images illustrate the six Cape Town/Western Cape concept briefs. They are not evidence of the named sites, local commissions or indigenous plant species. Original asset filenames are retained to avoid unnecessary binary churn: riverton → Waterfront; carter → Woodstock; commons → Century City; maple → Stellenbosch; northpoint → Foreshore; ellis → Claremont. The generated hero is an imagined interior, not a photograph of Cape Town. The original concept board records Phase 1 art direction; current desktop/mobile previews show the repositioned implementation.
